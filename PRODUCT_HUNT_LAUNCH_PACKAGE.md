### Description Complète (Corps de la page Product Hunt)
> **The Problem :**
> Trade contractors (plumbers, HVAC techs, electricians, roofers) work with their hands. When a pipe bursts at 8 PM, a homeowner dials the first plumber on Google.
> If the plumber is under a sink and can't answer, the homeowner doesn't leave a voicemail — they hang up and dial the next competitor. Plumbers lose between $450 and $2,500 on every single missed call.
>
> **The Solution : CallCatch AI**
> CallCatch AI is an AI-powered revenue recovery system that takes 2 minutes to set up:
> 1. **Zero-Friction Call Forwarding :** Whenever an artisan misses a call, CallCatch AI instantly intercepts it.
> 2. **< 5-Second Text-Back :** The caller receives an immediate, polite text from the contractor's AI assistant.
> 3. **Conversational AI Triage :** The AI detects the exact issue (burst pipe, no heat, electrical trip), extracts the property address, and secures the dispatch slot.
> 4. **Private Dashboard :** The artisan gets a real-time summary on mobile and can take over or dispatch their crew.
>

> - ⚡ Under 5-second response time.
